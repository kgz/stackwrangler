IAM Access Analyzer for S3

When a bucket policy, Access Point policy, or ACL is added or modified, IAM Access Analyzer generates and updates findings based on the change within 30 minutes. Findings related to account-level Block public access settings or Multi-Region Access Point configurations may not be generated or updated for up to 6 hours after you change the settings.
